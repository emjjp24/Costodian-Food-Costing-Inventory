About Us

A simple tool made for people who cook and sell food, not for accountants.

Costodian is a food costing and inventory system. Many home cooks and small food sellers guess their prices, and only find out an ingredient ran out when they already started cooking.

We built Costodian to fix that. You enter your recipes once, and the system works out the cost of each dish, suggests a price that protects your profit, and keeps your ingredient stock up to date as you cook.

It understands the way people really measure: halves, quarters and mixed numbers like 1 1/2, not only decimals.

Personal use Home cooking, baking and meal prep. Know what your meals cost and what is left in the kitchen.
Small business A stall, a bakery or a carinderia. Price your menu and watch your stock and waste.

What Costodian does for you

Everything you need to cost a dish and keep your pantry in order.

Recipe costing

See the cost of the whole batch and of every serving, with your own ingredient prices.

Automatic inventory

Ingredients you enter while costing are added to your inventory by themselves.

Stock goes down as you cook

Cook a batch and the ingredients you used are taken out of your stock for you.

Low stock and expiry alerts

Get warned before an ingredient runs low or goes bad.

Real-life measurements

Price suggestions
