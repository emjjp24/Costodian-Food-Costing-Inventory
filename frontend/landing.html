About Us

A simple tool made for people who cook and sell food, not for accountants.

Costodian is a food costing and inventory system. Many home cooks and small food sellers guess their prices, and only find out an ingredient ran out when they already started cooking.

We built Costodian to fix that. You enter your recipes once, and the system works out the cost of each dish, suggests a price that protects your profit, and keeps your ingredient stock up to date as you cook.

It understands the way people really measure: halves, quarters and mixed numbers like 1 1/2, not only decimals.

Questions people ask

Short answers before you sign up.

Is it really free?

Yes. You can create an account and use every tab without paying.

Do I have to type in my ingredients one by one?

Only once. When you add a recipe, new ingredients are saved to your inventory automatically, and next time you pick them from a list.

What if the price of an ingredient changes?

Restock it with the new price. Costodian uses the average of what you paid, so your recipe costs stay fair.

Can other people see my recipes?

No. Each account has its own recipes, inventory and history, and only you can see them when you are signed in.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The pages fit small screens, with compact lists and a details panel when you tap an item.
